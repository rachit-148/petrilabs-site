The 45-minute class

What happens in the class?

An age-old, time-tested five-step cycle of learning — proven long before the modern classroom.

0–5 min

Jigyasa

The hook

Teacher sparks curiosity with a question, using the 5-minute prep guide.

5–15 min

Bodh

The understanding

The core concept is unpacked and linked back to the textbook.

15–25 min

Abhyas

The practice

Guided practice to reinforce the concept before applying it.

25–40 min

Prayog

The experiment

Students unbox the mapped kit and run the experiment on their desks.

40–45 min

Prasar

The connection

Connect the concept across subjects and to current affairs — cross-curricular reach beyond the chapter.
