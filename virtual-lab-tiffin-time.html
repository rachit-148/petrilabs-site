Tiffin Time: what does your food do inside you?

You are the tiffin helper. Pick foods for the plate, check they are clean, then eat and watch your body fill up with energy, growth and protection.

What you will find out

Which foods give you energy, which help you grow, and which keep you from falling sick; how to check food is safe before you eat it; and why taking a little and finishing it is better than a big plate with leftovers.

How this lab works

  1. 1In Thali, tap foods to put them on the plate. Look carefully — some foods are dirty or spoiled and belong in the bin. The bottom picture shows what each food does inside you.
  2. 2Say how hungry you are, then tap Eat! and see if you finish your plate or leave food behind.
  3. 3In Games, play Would you eat this?, What does it do? and Complete the thali.
  4. 4Answer three questions as you go, then finish with a summary.

Teacher notes

Foundational Stage · Physical Development · CG-1 · C-1.1 "Shows a liking for and understanding of nutritious food and does not waste food". Best used with an adult at the screen for ages 3–6, and independently at 6–8.

  • Predict first: ask "Which food helps you run fast — roti or milk?" and "What happens to a roti that falls on the floor?" Note the answers, then let children test them here.
  • Real tiffin check: at snack time, children sort their own tiffin into Energy, Growth and Protective foods, and check it is covered and clean (C1.1-S1-L4, S2-L4).
  • Rubric coverage: the games are the auto-scored items in the rubric. Would you eat this? = S1-L1/L2, What does it do? = S2-L1/L2, Complete the thali = S2-L3/L4, building a plate in Thali = S2-L5, and Eat! with the hunger setting = S4-L4 "Serve it right". Trying and enjoying food (S3) and mealtime habits stay as observation checklists.
