What the learner must KNOW for this performance to be possible. Sequenced by conceptual dependence, not by difficulty.
The Spine is Layer 1 of the 3-layer model — book- and grade-agnostic. Curricular Goals and Competencies are reproduced verbatim from the National Curriculum Framework for School Education (NCF-SE) 2023, Part C, Ch 3 §3.4.1.1. Learning Trajectories, Performance Threads and Performance Outcomes are authored on the model of the NCF flow-down examples and are illustrative & editable. Levels denote increasing sophistication across the stage — deliberately not pinned to a grade (that mapping lives in Layer 2).
